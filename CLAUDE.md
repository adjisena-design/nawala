# Nawala: the living letter

Home page for Nawala, a digital wedding invitation business in Indonesia. Static site, no build step: `index.html`, `assets/css/site.css`, `assets/js/site.js`. Served by GitHub Pages from `main` at https://nawalainvite.com/ since 21 Nov 2026 (the `CNAME` file; DNS is at Cloudflare, two DNS-only CNAME records to `adjisena-design.github.io`, and the mail records for Zoho beside them). Asset paths stay relative, so the site works from any folder.

## Standing rules from the owner
- Every design update is committed and pushed to `main` without asking. This repository is public.
- Speed is as important as looks. Most guests open Nawala on low and middle-spec Android phones; lag means they leave. Animate only transform and opacity. Glass blur switches off on small screens; everything that moves stops under reduced motion.
  - One agreed exception: the desktop menu morph (top bar into left rail) moves `clip-path` on two small glass shapes. It only exists at 900px and wider. Phones do the same job with opacity and scale.
- No emojis anywhere. No real or generated human faces: Nawala (the winged letter) stands in for the couple.
- Site copy is Indonesian first with an English toggle. Every visible string carries `data-en`; the script swaps it. Strings built in `site.js` go through `t(id, en)`.
- Nothing thin and nothing tiny: body text 17px or larger, touch targets 44px or larger.
- Do not invent evidence. There are no customers, testimonials or prices yet. The 50 theme covers and 20 sample films are real. Working sample invitations exist for the General Nawala theme, Pendopo Jawa, Akad Putih and Kyoto (Premium), Istana Awan, Teater Tua and Kapal Bulan (Eksklusif) and the 12 Dasar themes; the other 25 Premium and 7 Eksklusif covers still have no invitation behind them, and the page says so. No theme is marked as owned until a couple has really bought it. The log-in box checks codes against the database; if the check cannot be reached it says so and offers WhatsApp.
- Higgsfield credits (owner, 21 Nov 2026, 12:40): spend down to 350 remaining, then stop and ask. (Earlier the line was 500.) Balance after the four journey worlds: 412.65.

## Look
Realism + Utopia. The photographs carry the colour; the interface stays quiet.
- Green belongs to the world, yellow to the light, pink only to Nawala and what it touches (glow borders, the contact box, the seal, blushing).
- Tokens are at the top of `site.css`. Display type is Fraunces, body is Plus Jakarta Sans, both self-hosted in `assets/fonts`. `?font=2` previews Bricolage Grotesque as the display face; `?h=2` and `?h=3` preview alternative hero headlines. These are review switches for the owner, remove them once he has chosen.
- No all-caps labels, no eyebrow labels above headings, no middle-dot taglines, no arrows inside buttons.

## The page is one descent
Sky, arch, trees, deep forest, flower hill. Each section sits on scenery; text sits on glass or on a shade over the picture.

| Altitude | Section | Scenery | Creatures (all drawn from cut-outs, moved with transforms) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `.hero` | open sky above the clouds, sun rays | two flocks of white doves, a small plane with a contrail, an ivory blimp |
| 2 | `.about` | moss arch | three doves |
| 3 | `.services` | treetops, warm golden green | two doves |
| 4 | `.themes` | mossy branches, dim + the river of covers | three butterflies, and the branch Nawala bumps into |
| 5 | `.contact` | boulder with small flowers | nine butterflies, bride and groom |

Parts:
- `.nav`: one set of links, laid out in rail position. On the hero they are moved into a top bar with transforms worked out in `layoutNav()`; `navMorph()` scrubs between the two with scroll. `.nav__top` and `.nav__side` are the two glass shapes. Phones: `.menu-btn` + slide-in `.nav__items`.
- `.nw`: the one travelling Nawala (fixed). `plan()` builds a list of waypoints from the section positions; `nwMove()` blends position, size and turn between them and sets the pose (`data-pose`: fly, tilt, back34, open, tumble, hop1 to hop3, rest, front). Flying is `nw-flap.webp`: one wing beat, 12 frames side by side, stepped with a transform. Cues: `flip` (3D turn showing its back), `bump` (flies into the tip of `#branch`, then tumbles). On the contact box it hops (crouch, air, land) and then rests. Tapping it makes it face you, blush and spin.
- `.sweep`: the light that carries one section into the next. Not one arch: four swells (`i` = glow, `s` = fill drawn on top, in step) wobble on their own clocks like a lava lamp made of light, with a faint spectrum on the edge and two faint arcs (`b`) lifting off. Coloured per section with `--veil`, `--rim`, `--g1`, `--g2`. `.fade-top` blends the fill into the scenery below it.
- `.about`: pinned stage. Three messages sit on the right over a white fade and glide upward with the scroll (`--y`, `--o` set per message in `measure()`); Nawala keeps to the left of the arch. On phones the fade and the messages are at the top.
- `.services`: on desktop the small screens move into one sticky phone and swap; on phones each sits inside its service card.
  - The five small screens are pages of one sample invitation (Laras & Bima, guest Dinda) in the **General Nawala theme** ("Tema Umum Nawala", the owner's name for Nawala's own look applied to an invitation: our scenery plates, Nawala, Fraunces, ivory cards, the pink seal). Opening on the arch, guestbook on the sky, floor plan on the treetops, souvenir on the boulder, gallery on the branches. In the desktop phone they share one tab bar (`#phone-tabs`), hidden on the opening. The floor plan is a drawn reception room (`floorPlan()` in `site.js`: stage, aisle, ten round tables of eight, buffet, gifts, entrance), standing up in the desktop phone and lying down in the phone-size service card.
- `.themes`: `.river` is built by script from every theme (covers drift diagonally, cannot be touched). The panel has four tier doors; each opens `dialog#pop-themes` on that tab.
  - Tiers (data at the top of `site.js`): `BASIC` (Dasar, 12), `THEMES` (Premium, 28 in seven families), `EXCLUSIVE` (Eksklusif, 10), and Kustom, which is a four-step process with its own WhatsApp message. `ALL` is the combined list.
  - Detail view: cover, "Putar film" when the slug is in `FILMS` (`assets/video/<slug>.mp4`, 720 px wide, silent, about 0.1 to 1 MB), the sample couple, "Gunakan tema ini".
  - Films only ever start from a tap, and are not offered on Save-Data or 2G/3G. Exclusive films need an access code; `?film=1` still unlocks them for the owner's review. He can now make himself a code in the team console, so remove the switch once he says he has one.
  - Exclusive themes are sold to one couple. Add the slug to `OWNED` after a real sale: the card is marked "Sudah dimiliki" and can no longer be chosen. It is empty on purpose.
  - "Gunakan tema ini" carries the choice into the contact box; the end of each list asks for a WhatsApp registration.
- `dialog#pop-login`: access code only. Connected since 21 Nov 2026: it calls `check_access_code` and, on yes, keeps the code in `sessionStorage` (`nawala-code`), which opens the Exclusive films, and offers a link to the couple's room (`ruang/`), which reads the same key.
- `.foot__doors`: two quiet links in the footer, to `ruang/` and `tim/`.
- `.contact`: one box, three questions (name, wedding month and year, WhatsApp number), then an animated tick. `.pair` is bride and groom Nawala: they hop toward each other, then keep playing on an 8 second loop (blush, jump, turn white, circle each other, blush again).
- Sections get `.is-live` while on or near the screen (creatures and light only move then) and `.is-near` once close (background pictures, masks and the river load then).

Scroll effects read two CSS variables that `site.js` sets on every `[data-scroll]` section: `--e` (0 to 1 while the section enters) and `--p` (0 to 1 while it passes).

## Consoles and the database (since 21 Nov 2026)
Two pages beside the home page, both `noindex`, both the owner's designs brought in from his claude.ai artifacts and put on real data. Nothing in them is a sample; an empty database shows empty lists.

| Page | Opens with | Files |
| --- | --- | --- |

Shared: `assets/css/console.css` (dark glass among the treetops for the team, ivory glass above the clouds for the couple; the door is `.gate`, shown while `body.is-locked`), `assets/js/console.js` (`window.K`: language, views by `#name`, toast, tooltip, dates), `assets/js/themes.js` (`window.NAWALA`: the theme lists; **the same lists are at the top of `site.js`, change both**), `assets/js/db.js` (`window.NAWALA_DB.rpc(name, body)`, the project URL and the publishable key).

How access works: no table can be read or written from outside. Every call is a function in `20261121_consoles.sql` (security definer, `search_path` empty) that checks what it is handed.
- **Team token.** Made only in the dashboard's SQL editor, where it is shown once: `select private.new_team_token('Nama');` gives an existing member a new token (the old one stops working), `select private.new_team_token('Nama Baru', 'operator');` adds a member. Claude never makes, sees or stores one: the owner runs that line himself. To end someone's access: `update public.team_members set ended_at = now() where name = 'Nama';`. The console keeps the token in `sessionStorage`, or in `localStorage` when "Ingat saya di perangkat ini" is ticked. Both roles can do everything in the console today; the role is shown, and reserved for later limits.
- **The door's lock** (`20261121_team_door.sql`). The owner found the first 64-character tokens impossible to remember and asked for something like `NAWALA-02985-TIM`. Five digits could be guessed in under an hour, so the answer was a short random token plus a limit on guessing: tokens are `NAWALA-TIM-XXXXXX` (six characters from 32, capitals or small letters); five wrong tries from one address close the door to that address for 15 minutes, a right token clears the count; fifty wrong tries from everywhere in 15 minutes close it to everyone (one attacker, many addresses). That leaves about 4,800 guesses a day against a billion possibilities. The address comes from the gateway's `cf-connecting-ip` header. Because an error would undo the row that counts a try, a refused call no longer raises: `team_data` answers `{denied: 'salah' | 'terkunci', sisa}` and the writing functions answer null. While the door is locked the console keeps a remembered token, so the team gets back in without retyping. To open the door early: `delete from private.door_tries;`.
- **Access code.** Made in the console (Kode): valid 7, 14 or 30 days (30 is the default) or with no end date. The owner's rule: when a couple needs more time they contact us, and we extend it, without limit if we choose. So each code has "Tambah 30 hari", "Tanpa batas waktu", "Tandai jadi pesanan" and "Akhiri sekarang" (an ended code opens again when extended). A code made from a request is linked to that lead, and the new-code box offers a ready WhatsApp message with the code and `nawalainvite.com/ruang/`.
- **Couple's room.** `room_open` checks the code and its end date and returns the label, the dates and the saved state; `room_save` takes every change (saved themes up to 30, passed themes, the five match answers, the final choice, the requested video-call time) about a second after the last tap. Two phones on one code: the last change wins. A code that ends while the couple is inside sends them back to the door at the next save, with a WhatsApp link to ask for more time. The room remembers the code in `localStorage` so the couple need not type it each visit.
- **Team console views.** Ringkasan (requests this week, waiting, in work, active codes, the five stages, video calls couples asked for, requests per week, most chosen themes: all counted from the rows), Permintaan (reply opens WhatsApp with the lead's number and moves it to Dihubungi; next stage, back a stage, make a code), Undangan (register a live invitation's slug, switch its answers on or off, hide or show wishes), Tema (film, sample invitation, times chosen; read only, because what the site shows is still decided in `site.js`), Kode.
- **Live invitations.** A couple file with `sample: false` and a `slug` registered in the console saves answers (`guest_rsvp`) and wishes (`guest_wish`) and shows the wishes the team has not hidden (`guest_wishes`). The row ids are kept on the guest's phone, so changing an answer changes the same row. If saving fails, or answers are switched off, the button turns into the WhatsApp link it always was. Samples (`sample: true`, which is every invitation in the repository today) never save anything and keep the WhatsApp link. No real couple's invitation exists yet.
- Tables: `leads` (now with `theme`, `stage`, `stage_at`), `team_members`, `private.door_tries`, `access_codes` (`expires_at` null means no end date; `state` is the room's json), `invitations`, `rsvps`, `wishes`.
- Checked 21 Nov 2026: the whole migration and every function on a local Postgres 16 as `anon`; both consoles, a second device, expiry and the invitation paths in a test browser against that local database (1440x900 and 390x844, both languages); then on the real project with the publishable key: every team function refuses a made-up token (403), table reads are refused (401), a made-up code is false, an unregistered invitation saves nothing, a lead with a theme inserts (201) and a forged stage is refused; a self-test inside the database made a token and a code and rolled itself back. Test rows were removed; the real tables are empty. Not yet done with a real token in the live console: that needs the owner's token.
- Not done yet: the lock covers team tokens only (an access code has 32^8 possibilities and no lock-out); no email or push tells the team about a new request, they see it in the console; the console cannot yet mark an Exclusive theme as owned or hide a theme on the site (both are still edits to `site.js` and `themes.js`).

## The Premium invitation (`undangan/`), since 21 Nov 2026
The first working invitation: Laras & Bima in the General Nawala theme, at `undangan/` (live: `https://nawalainvite.com/undangan/?to=Nama+Tamu`). The home page links to it from the first service card. The 50 theme covers still have no invitation pages of their own. The owner's instruction: get this one right, then replicate it to the others.

It was rebuilt the same day after the owner's review (ivory cards, free scrolling and no light between scenes read as less premium than his reference reels). What his 11 reference reels share, and what this build follows: a camera journey; the writing stands inside the world on a clear sheet with flowers over its base; one composed beat at a time; the practical parts after the film.

Four parts, so that a new theme or a new couple is one new file each:
- `skeleton.css`: the structure shared by every theme.
- `theme-umum.css`: one theme kit. Per scene (`[data-scene]`): `--plate` (720 x 1290), `--fg` (front layer), `--fg-line`, `--tone`, `--shade`, and the light that brings the scene in (`--veil`, `--rim`, `--g1`, `--g2`, the home page's own values). Plus ornaments (doves, fireflies, butterflies, gallery frames). A new theme is a new `theme-<name>.css` and its pictures.
- `couple-laras-bima.js`: one invitation. Names, date, events, story, gifts, sample wishes, `theme`, `level`, `wording`, `whatsapp`, `music`. It is loaded in the head, before the kit is chosen. Guest-facing text is `[Indonesian, English]`.
- `invite.js`: the engine.

How it works:
- **Guided walk, not a scrolling page.** `.stage` is fixed; each `.step` fills it; `go()` moves exactly one step for any swipe (short or hard), wheel notch, arrow key, the round next button or a bottom-bar jump, and ignores input until the move has finished. There is no native page scroll. A panel taller than a very short screen scrolls inside itself, and a swipe only changes step once that panel is at its end. One step per event is built from the couple file (`#event-step` template).
- **Panels are clear acrylic sheets** (`.pane`): a light gradient, bright edge and sheen, no blur on phones (blur only at 900px and wider). A panel has no bottom edge: a still mask fades the glass, its border and sheen out before the front layer begins, because the owner noticed the hard line where a panel's base met the flowers (RSVP, digital envelope, closing). Watch for any box or motion that ends in a sharp cut; he notices them. `data-tone="dark"` on a step gives light writing on dim scenery, `light` gives dark writing.
- **Scenery and its front layer.** `.world` (plate) and `.front` (the plate's own moss, ferns, flowers or peaks, cut out as `assets/img/fg-<scene>-mobile-sm.webp`) share one box (`.fit`: covers the stage, stands on the bottom edge), so they line up. Order: world, steps, fronts, light, Nawala. `sinks()` gives every panel bottom padding (`--sink`) so writing stays above the front layer; it is never under 46px because the next button sits there. The camera pushes in a little at every step inside a scene (`--cam`, `--camf`).
- **Light between scenes** (`.sweep`): the home page's lava-lamp swells and spectrum rim, run on a clock instead of scroll. It rises (or comes down when going back), the scenery is swapped while the screen is covered, and the next panel arrives as it leaves. Only on a change of scene.
- **Nawala travels** (`.nw`): `data-nw` on a step says where it goes: `fly:x,y,size`, `perch:x` (sits on the panel's top edge, folded wings), `hover:x`, `hide`. It always finishes its flight because steps cannot be half-way. After an RSVP is written out it opens and lets hearts out.
- **Opening**: Nawala shows the address side with the guest's name (`?to=`), turns to the seal, opens; then the film (`assets/video/umum-opening.mp4`: through the arch into the sky, first frame = arch plate, last frame = sky plate) plays at 1.2x if it is already buffered at the tap, otherwise the still arch zooms. A skip button shows during the film. Music starts on that same tap, never on Save-Data or 2G.
- The stage height is pinned in pixels (`--sh`) so the on-screen keyboard cannot squeeze the scenery; `lift()` raises the steps so the field being typed in stays above the keyboard. Not yet tried on a real phone.

Rules specific to the invitation:
- Levels (the owner's three-level skeleton; names and contents are drafts): 1 Dasar = cover, greeting, bride, groom, countdown, events, RSVP, closing (9 steps). 2 Menengah adds music, calendar button, gallery, wishes, digital envelope. 3 Maksimal adds story, dress colours, live stream (14 steps). Steps and blocks carry `data-level`. "Maksimal" means every section is on; it does not mean the most premium production. Production value is the theme tier (Dasar, Premium, Eksklusif, Kustom).
- Wording sets live in `WORDING` in `invite.js`: `umum` (neutral) and `islam` (salam, Ar-Rum 21, akad nikah). Chosen per couple. The religious wording still needs the owner's review.
- RSVP on a sample: the send button is a plain WhatsApp link whose message is rewritten as the guest types, and the page only says the answer is written out in WhatsApp. RSVP on a live invitation (see "Consoles and the database"): the answer and the wish are saved, the page says so, and WhatsApp is the way out if saving fails. A guest's own wish is kept on their phone and shown as "dari Anda". Sample wishes are tagged "contoh".
- No human faces: bride and groom Nawala stand in for the couple; the gallery is one large frame and six thumbnails, each the cut-outs set into the scenery with CSS. Real couples' photos replace them later.
- Sample invitations (`sample: true`) show a "Contoh" button that opens a review sheet (level, wording, guest name, replay the opening) and a note in the closing. Real invitations show neither.
- Review links: `?tema=umum|pendopo-jawa|akad-putih|istana-awan|teater-tua|kapal-bulan|kyoto` (samples only), `?to=`, `?lang=en`, `?level=1|2|3`, `?salam=umum|islam`, `?step=<id>` (start at a step).
- Film and music are in the repository since 21 Nov 2026: `assets/video/umum-opening.mp4` (720 x 1294, 5 s, 0.76 MB, from Higgsfield job `0e8abc71-5e02-4b65-ab66-900a5c3eda24`) and `assets/audio/nawala-umum.mp3` (60 s, 96 kbps, 0.72 MB, from job `baeb642f-d452-4ef1-a05e-34c308d5f950`). How files arrive: the workspace and the Mac sandbox cannot reach Higgsfield, so each file is downloaded through the owner's Chrome (one download per page load, or Chrome blocks it), lands in Chrome's own download folder, and the owner moves it into `Downloads/Nawala`, where it is shrunk with ffmpeg into `generated/nawala-pass4/web/` and staged into the repo.

  - **Journeys (owner, 21 Nov 2026: every world should not move the same way).** A kit may name a `journey` in `KITS`; then every change of scene moves in that world's own direction instead of the light sweep, and the opening film moves the same way. `travel()` in `invite.js` puts both scenes on screen (`.j-out`, `.j-in`) and runs `#seam` over the join; the keyframes are in "Journeys" at the end of `skeleton.css`; only transform and opacity move; under reduced motion the scene is swapped at once. The first three kits have no journey and keep the light sweep (General Nawala: down from the sky; Pendopo Jawa and Akad Putih: in through a gate or curtain).
    - `up` (Istana Awan): the new scene comes down from above, the old one leaves below, a band of cloud rides on the join. Film: climbing the marble stairs.
    - `side` (Teater Tua): scenes pass sideways like frames of an old film; a soft dark frame line rides on the join and perforations run along the top and bottom edge while it moves; panels slide sideways too; the kit lays fine flickering grain and a vignette over the scenery. Film: out of the wings onto the stage.
    - `diagonal` (Kapal Bulan): the new scene drifts in from the upper right over the old one (both a little enlarged so no edge shows) and a slanting band of light crosses; panels move diagonally. Film: the boat leaves the jetty up and across.
