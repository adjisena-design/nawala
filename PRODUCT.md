# Product

## What Nawala is
Nawala is an old Javanese word for a letter. Nawala sells digital wedding invitations in Indonesia: a personal link for each guest that opens with a living, winged letter carrying their name, then leads them into the couple's theme. One line: Surat yang hidup, the living letter.

## Users
Engaged couples in Indonesia and the family members helping them, usually on a phone, usually comparing vendors on WhatsApp and Instagram. Their guests open the invitation on low and middle-spec Android phones.

## What is offered
- Digital invitation with the guest's name on the opening envelope, RSVP, map, countdown and digital gift.
- QR guestbook at the entrance.
- A wedding-day web page opened by QR, with seat finder, floor plan, menu, guest photo gallery and souvenir collection.
- Themes: 500 to 1,000 planned for January 2027, from seven families (nature, royal, faith, Nusantara, the world, eras, elements). 100 to 250 will be open to browse; the rest unlock after the visitor registers by WhatsApp and receives a token.
- Special themes made for one couple, and paid edits by a Nawala editor. There is no free-form canvas editor.

## Principles
1. Every guest is met personally.
2. Fast and smooth on a cheap phone, or it does not ship.
3. Nawala stands in for the couple; no borrowed faces.
4. Ask for as little as possible: three questions, then WhatsApp.
