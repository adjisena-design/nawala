# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, one self-contained file (the user asked for "a html").

## Users

Engaged couples in Indonesia (starting with Bandung and Jakarta) and the family members helping them, choosing how to invite guests and run guest management for a wedding. They compare vendors on WhatsApp and Instagram, usually on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Nawala (working name) sells digital wedding invitations built as layered, painted-looking vector scenes, plus the event-day services that usually come from separate vendors: RSVP chasing by a human team, QR guestbook with crew and tablets, and live streaming. Success: a visitor browses the templates, finds one that feels like their wedding, and starts one.

## Positioning

- One booking covers invitation, RSVP assistance, guestbook and streaming, with prices published up front and no crossed-out "normal" prices (competitors Viding, Tibra Digital, Invitato, Katsudoto all use struck prices or hide prices behind WhatsApp).
- The invitations look like layered paper or oil paintings with real depth, not flat template cards.
- Shows hosts who has opened their invitation (few competitors offer this).

## Operating Context

Invitations are sent by WhatsApp with a per-guest name link. Guests open them on phones. Events often have separate sessions (akad, resepsi). Gifts go by bank transfer or e-wallet (amplop digital). Event day uses QR check-in, table plans and a greeting screen.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Invitation packages (proposed, user-approved as draft offer): Sketsa Rp199rb, Lukisan Rp449rb, Mahakarya Rp799rb.
- Event-day services (proposed): Asisten RSVP Rp990rb, Buku Tamu QR Rp1,79jt, Live Streaming Rp3,49jt, Paket Utuh Rp5,9jt.
- Wedding organizer / day-of coordination: planned, not yet offered.
- Starting templates: Taman Senja (garden, pendopo), Fuji Hanami, Paris Lumière, Alpen Kecil, Pantai Bulan.
- Site copy in Bahasa Indonesia.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Nawala (working name, not locked; alternatives Rona, Senja, Kirana).
- Logo direction: 3D marble wordmark in soft colours (reference: LAVA AI logo), flattened so it does not look like a balloon; one uniform logo pack.
- Light theme: soft orange, white, light blue accent. Dark theme: deep navy / blue. No purple or violet anywhere (user rejected it as looking generated).
- Visual references the user made binding: ieef.or.id (textured vector scenes, arch frames, glass cards over full-bleed art) but lighter, with deeper shadows and more translucency; elevenlabs.io for overall cleanliness.
- Headlines: Instrument Serif with one italic phrase (user chose this from Dribbble references on 14 Nov); body and logo stay Plus Jakarta Sans. Hero: full-screen vector valley scene with centred text.
- No emojis. No dotted "status" chips or middle-dot taglines.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real couples, photos, testimonials, client logos, usage numbers or press. Do not fabricate any of them; use the vector artwork and marked slots.

## Product Principles

1. The invitation is the hero: show templates, not claims.
2. Honest, published prices.
3. One booking, one team, from invitation to event day.
4. Warm and human, never gimmicky.
